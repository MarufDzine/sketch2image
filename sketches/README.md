Put your own sketches here as `sketch1.png`, `sketch2.png`, `sketch3.png` (PNG or JPG).

Tips for good results:
- Dark pen/marker on plain white paper, thick-ish clear lines.
- Photograph from straight above in even light (no strong shadows); crop to the drawing.
- Then edit `subject:` for each sketch in `config.yaml` to describe what you drew.
